Weighted score: **8.25/10**, displayed as **8.3/10**. The level-design category is below the minimum completion floor of 8.0, and the weighted score is below 9.0. **Goal remains active.**

## What must improve next

1. Give the middle trials different encounter objectives and route rhythms, not only different wall layouts and enemy mixes. Preserve speed while making the radial, beam and pursuit themes matter in play.
2. Make advanced mastery goals reward specific skill demonstrations, with fair windows and clear feedback. Do not add challenge through invisible damage, arbitrary waiting or enemy health inflation.
3. Inspect complete updated runs and the actual mixed audio after every substantial change. Keep the source, recordings and playable checkpoint synchronized.
4. Re-run the relevant regression, ordinary-input completion and sustained player checks. Passing them remains a prerequisite, not an automatic score increase.

The editor activation limitation and native capture/input-tool limitations remain documented. Neither a universal 9/10 rating nor an external human playtest is being claimed.
